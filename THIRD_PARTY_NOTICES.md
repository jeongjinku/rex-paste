# Third-party notices

The release binaries statically link the following libraries. Their licenses follow.

- macOS binary: [libpng](http://www.libpng.org/pub/png/libpng.html) and [libwebp](https://chromium.googlesource.com/webm/libwebp) (including libsharpyuv and libwebpmux), from Homebrew.
- Linux binaries: [musl libc](https://musl.libc.org/), as shipped with Zig 0.17.0.
- All binaries: parts of the [Zig](https://ziglang.org/) standard library and compiler-rt.
